# Al-Kitab Bot

Al-Kitab is an Islamic Discord bot for prayer times and reminders, Quran reading and recitation, Hadith, prayer tracking, and more. Use it in DMs or add it to a server for shared schedules.

## Add Al-Kitab

[Add the bot to your Discord app or server](https://discord.com/oauth2/authorize?client_id=1184171513075732520)

After adding it, type `/bot help` in Discord to browse the available commands. Commands beginning with `/server` manage a server and require **Manage Server** permission.

## Features and commands

### Prayer times, reminders, and tracking

- `/prayer-times manage` — Set your location, calculation preferences, and time format with guided setup.
- `/prayer-times today` — View today's prayer times from your saved settings.
- `/prayer-reminders` — Set up or manage personal prayer reminders, including pause, resume, skip next, and restore next.
- `/prayers mark`, `/prayers unmark`, and `/prayers status` — Record and review prayers.
- `/prayers follow-ups` — Manage prayer tracker follow-ups.
- `/prayers forbidden-times` — Check times when voluntary prayer is forbidden.
- `/server prayer-reminders` — Manage prayer reminders in a server channel.

### Quran

- `/quran verse` and `/quran random` — Read a selected or random verse in English or Arabic.
- `/quran surahs` — Browse the Surahs.
- `/bookmarks set` and `/bookmarks show` — Save and view your Quran reading position.
- `/daily-verse` and `/server daily-verse` — Manage daily verse delivery by DM or in a server channel.
- `/recitation play` — Play a Surah by number or name in voice chat. Use `/recitation queue`, `/recitation now-playing`, `/recitation seek`, `/recitation pause`, `/recitation resume`, `/recitation skip`, `/recitation clear`, and `/recitation leave` to control playback.

### Hadith and Islamic calendar

- `/hadith get` and `/hadith random` — Read Hadith in English, Arabic, or Urdu.
- `/daily-hadith` and `/server daily-hadith` — Manage daily Hadith delivery.
- `/hijri convert` and `/hijri today` — Convert a Gregorian date or view today's Hijri date.
- `/hijri settings`, `/hijri update`, and `/hijri delete-settings` — Manage Hijri calendar preferences.
- `/fasting-reminders` and `/server fasting-reminders` — Manage reminders for Monday and Thursday or Ayyam al-Beed fasting.
- `/allah-names list` and `/allah-names show` — Explore the Names of Allah.

### Bot and data controls

- `/bot help` — Browse commands and usage guidance.
- `/bot vote`, `/bot vote-stats`, `/bot leaderboard`, and `/bot accents` — Vote, view verified vote rewards, and choose an accent color.
- `/vote-reminders` and `/server vote-reminders` — Manage vote reminders.
- `/my-data settings` — Review your saved personal settings.
- `/my-data delete` — Delete your saved personal data after confirmation.
- `/server settings` and `/server delete` — Review or delete a server's saved bot settings and schedules.

Subscription menus show your current settings and offer update and disable controls. Disabling or deleting data requires confirmation. Server commands require **Manage Server** permission.

## Example usage

```text
/prayer-times manage
/prayer-reminders
/quran random
/recitation play
/hadith random
/prayers status
```
